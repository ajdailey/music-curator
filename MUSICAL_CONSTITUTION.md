# Musical Constitution

The curation principles the engine implements. Other documents cite these by
number; do not renumber them.

1. **Album preservation.** Complete albums remain available in the collection
   even when playlists draw from them in non-album order.
2. **Taxonomy and sequencing are separate.** A genre determines membership; an
   ordering strategy determines how it plays.
3. **Version preference.** Studio versions are the default. Meaningful live
   versions are kept, not discarded.
4. **Artist rotation.** Curated and genre playlists rotate artists and albums
   so no artist occupies a long stretch.
5. **Deep cuts.** Generation must not collapse complete collections into
   greatest-hits programming.
6. **Deterministic sequencing.** Regeneration preserves a playlist's character
   and a broadly stable order.
7. **Portable output is curated.** Rockbox output is always intersected with
   `iPod Sync.m3u8` and never assumes the full Plex library fits.
8. **Album flow is explicit.** Complete album and mini-album blocks are used
   only by playlists that request them.
9. **The library reflects its owner's taste.** Generic genre definitions are a
   starting point, not the final authority.
10. **Uninterrupted listening.** Variety, pacing, version control, and artist
    spacing support long listening without skips.
